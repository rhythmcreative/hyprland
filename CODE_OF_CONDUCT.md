# Code of Conduct

Welcome to **rhythm's hyprland**. 

This project started as a deeply personal rice—crafted with love, countless late nights of tinkering, and an obsessive attention to aesthetic detail. Sharing it with the world is an invitation to anyone who loves Linux desktops, Wayland compositors, and creative workflows. 

Because of that, the atmosphere around this project matters just as much as the code itself. We want everyone who stops by—whether you are opening your first issue, submitting a pull request, asking how Quickshell works, or porting configurations to a new distribution—to feel respected, valued, and welcome.

---

## 1. Our Pledge

We are committed to making this project a safe, encouraging, and enjoyable space for everyone, regardless of:
- Experience level (from seasoned kernel hackers to someone installing their very first window manager)
- Operating system or distribution preference (we embrace Arch, Fedora, Debian, Ubuntu, Alpine, openSUSE, NixOS, and beyond without elitism)
- Background, race, ethnicity, culture, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or age

---

## 2. What We Value (The Human Side)

- **Empathy and patience first:** Everyone had a "Day 1" with Linux, Hyprland, and shell scripting. If someone is struggling with a monitor config, a graphic driver issue, or an install flag, help them with patience. We do not do "RTFM" or dismissive replies here.
- **Kindness in feedback:** Code reviews and design discussions should be collaborative, not adversarial. Critique ideas, configurations, and scripts—never the person behind them.
- **Generosity with knowledge:** Share tips, explain the *why* behind a tweak, and help others learn. The magic of the Linux community is collective discovery.
- **Respect for people's time:** Maintainers and contributors are volunteers who balance open source with real life, jobs, families, and rest. Let's be mindful and grateful for each other's contributions.

---

## 3. What We Do Not Tolerate

We have zero tolerance for behaviors that degrade our community:
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Gatekeeping or distro elitism (e.g. mocking users of Debian, Ubuntu, Arch, or any other distro).
- Harassment of any kind, whether public in discussions/issues or private in messages.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission (doxxing).
- Sexualized language, inappropriate imagery, or unwelcome physical or sexual advances.

---

## 4. Resolving Conflicts & Reporting

We are human, and misunderstandings happen. When they do, our first approach is open, calm, and respectful communication.

However, if you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, or if you feel uncomfortable or unsafe:
- Reach out privately to **rhythmcreative** via GitHub or email at `rhythmcreative@users.noreply.github.com`.
- Every report will be treated with confidentiality, care, and an open mind.
- We will review the situation promptly and take fair, necessary steps—such as giving a private warning, editing or removing offensive comments, or, in severe cases, temporary or permanent exclusion from the repository.

---

Thank you for bringing your creativity, curiosity, and kindness to **rhythm's hyprland**! 💫
